About Me

Contemporary Artist & Visual Storyteller

Anand Buri

Born in the vibrant landscapes of Rajasthan, I discovered my passion for art at the tender age of seven, finding solace in the interplay of colors and forms that surrounded me.

My artistic journey has been a continuous exploration of human emotions, cultural narratives, and the sublime beauty hidden in everyday moments. Each brushstroke carries the weight of tradition while reaching toward contemporary expression.

With over fifteen years of dedicated practice, my work has been exhibited in galleries across Mumbai, Delhi, London, and New York. I believe art is not merely decoration—it is a dialogue between the soul and the universe.

My practice spans oil paintings, charcoal sketches, and digital illustrations, each medium offering a unique voice to express the ineffable.
